# SEO Research

Running log of keyword research, insights, and SEO strategy for Typer.

---

## Key insight: audience language gap

Typer's target users (non-technical Mac users) don't search using "local AI" terminology. That phrase has <100 monthly searches across all related keywords — the category barely exists as a search term yet.

They search from *pain points* and *familiar reference points* (ChatGPT, subscriptions, privacy). SEO strategy should meet them there.

---

## Keyword research log

### "Local AI" — Ahrefs, April 2026

All keywords returned <100 monthly volume. KD data mostly N/A (insufficient data).

**What the results revealed — three unrelated audiences mixed together:**

| Audience | Example keywords | Relevant to Typer? |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| AI tools for local businesses | "how can AI strengthen sales for local businesses" | No |
| Technical local AI setup | "how to run a local AI model", "how to setup local AI" | Partially — but low volume, skews technical |
| Local SEO + AI | "how can AI assist with local SEO optimization" | No |

**Takeaway:** "Local AI" is not how our audience thinks. Avoid anchoring SEO content to this term.

## Additional clusters — Ahrefs, April 2026

### ChatGPT ads
- `chatgpt ads` — Hard KD + >1,000 volume, but intent is **news**, not product search. Good narrative hook for Typer messaging, not a rankable SEO term.
- All specific variants (`chatgpt showing ads`, `chatgpt ads free users 2026`) — <100 volume. The story isn't driving search behaviour at scale.

### Competitor terms
- `ollama mac` — Hard KD + >100. Technical audience, hard to rank.
- `uninstall ollama` — Easy KD + >100. **Confirmed intercept opportunity.** People quitting Ollama (too complex). Content angle: *"Tried Ollama and gave up? Here's a local AI that works out of the box."*
- `uninstall ollama mac` — N/A KD + >100. Same audience, Mac-specific.
- `ollama alternative` — N/A KD + >100. More technical audience — lower priority for Typer.
- `lm studio mac` — N/A + >100. Technical audience.
- `jan ai mac` — N/A + <100. Low priority.

### Confirmed dead ends
- `run ai on mac`, `local ai mac`, `ai for mac free`, `ai that doesn't share data` — all <100. Regular users don't search in this language.

## Notes

- Do not target technical terms: "LLM", "inference", "Ollama", "quantization", model names
- Target pain-point language: running out of tokens, subscription fatigue, privacy concerns, offline use
- Mac-specific terms are a natural fit — Typer is Mac-only, and that's a filter, not a limitation
