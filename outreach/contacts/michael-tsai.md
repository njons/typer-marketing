## Outreach notes
Michael's blog format is link curation with commentary - he doesn't do long reviews, he points to things and adds his perspective. The best outcome is he links to Typer's website or a piece about it.

Email approach: brief, factual, no hype. "Built a free local AI chat app for Mac using Tauri and Rust. Might be of interest to your readers." Include the link. Let him decide.

Don't oversell. His audience is skeptical and knowledgeable. The facts are strong on their own: free, local, no account, web search without sending queries to servers.
