## Who they are

AppleInsider is a major Apple-focused publication with an 811,000-subscriber YouTube channel. The channel is hosted and produced primarily by Andrew O'Hara, who has been Chief Videographer since 2017. Publishes near-daily video content: hardware reviews, Mac tips, app coverage, comparison videos. The website has been running since 1997.

## Why relevant

AppleInsider actively covers Mac apps and productivity tools with hands-on video content. Andrew O'Hara personally hosts reviews with a genuine enthusiasm for finding tools that make Mac users more productive. A free, private, offline AI app with App Store distribution is a strong editorial hook: new category, zero cost to try, and it works on any Apple Silicon Mac. The ChatGPT ads narrative (February 2026) also gives it a timely news peg.

AppleInsider's ongoing Mac app coverage on their YouTube channel - search for their recent "mac apps" or "best mac apps" roundup videos. They also covered the macOS Sequoia release with dedicated tips and app feature videos.
