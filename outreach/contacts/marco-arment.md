## Why relevant
Under the Radar is a podcast specifically about "development, design, marketing and support for independent app development" - rarely longer than 30 minutes, released weekly. It's the gold standard indie dev podcast.

Marco has covered local AI, privacy in apps, the challenge of free vs. paid models, and the difficulty of sustaining indie development. A free-forever local AI app with no monetization plan is a genuinely interesting editorial angle for the show.

Separately, Marco's blog (marco.org) regularly covers app ecosystem issues from the developer perspective. He's written about the economics of free apps, App Store dynamics, and the indie dev landscape.

## Specific work to reference in outreach
