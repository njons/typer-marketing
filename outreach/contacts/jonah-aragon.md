## Outreach notes
Jonah reviews tools based on technical merits and data practices. Lead with the architecture: no account required, no telemetry, no cloud, and web search that also runs locally (unique). Don't lead with "I made an app" - lead with what makes it different architecturally. He may want to evaluate the app before writing about it, which is fine.

Privacy Guides has a formal tool submission process (GitHub), but a personal email to Jonah is more likely to get a thoughtful response. Keep it short and technical.
