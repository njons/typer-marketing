## Who they are

9to5Mac's YouTube channel has 935,000 subscribers and is one of the most-watched Apple-focused video channels online. Part of the 9to5 network (alongside 9to5Google, Electrek, etc.). The channel produces near-daily video content: Mac tips, app reviews, hardware news, iOS guides, and regular "apps worth trying" roundups. The network collectively generates 75M+ impressions per month.

## Why relevant

9to5Mac regularly publishes dedicated Mac app roundup videos (e.g. "60 Mac Tips for new M4 Mac setups" in November 2024, "These are the Mac apps I install first"). A free local AI app for Mac - especially with the "no cloud, no account, App Store download" story - is directly in their content wheelhouse. The ChatGPT ads narrative (February 2026) makes Typer especially timely right now.

## Specific piece to reference

"These are the Mac apps I install first" - an ongoing series on 9to5Mac covering essential Mac apps. Also their November 2024 "60 Mac Tips for M4 Mac users" video shows their appetite for Mac-focused how-to content.
