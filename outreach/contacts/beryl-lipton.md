## Why relevant
Lipton covers how "free" surveillance technology is never actually free - it trades user data for access. Her February 2026 piece on Amazon Ring is exactly the frame: surveillance tools marketed as beneficial that extract enormous amounts of data. Typer inverts this - AI software that is genuinely free AND collects nothing. The architecture is the story.

The Ring piece is the best hook: "free" software with a surveillance model. Typer is the answer to that question - what does free software look like when it actually means free?
