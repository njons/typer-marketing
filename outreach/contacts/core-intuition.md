# Core Intuition (Daniel Jalkut + Manton Reece)

## Overview
- **What they do**: Co-hosts of Core Intuition podcast - "indie software development for the Mac, iOS and other Apple technologies." Daniel Jalkut founded Red Sweater Software (MarsEdit, FlexiBits). Manton Reece founded Micro.blog.
- **Medium**: Podcast (Core Intuition)
- **Audience size**: Mid-tier. Long-running (since 2008), dedicated indie Mac dev audience. Not a massive mainstream show but respected and influential in the community.
- **Tier**: mid
- **Angle**: 3 (Indie Mac Dev Stories), 11 (Podcast hosts)

## Why relevant
Core Intuition is specifically about the business and craft of indie Mac development. Episodes cover App Store economics, marketing indie apps, building sustainable businesses, and the developer community. Daniel and Manton are both hands-on indie developers who talk shop.

The podcast recently went on hiatus and returned for special episodes about WWDC and AI developments - so they're still active for significant topics.

Typer's story touches several Core Intuition themes: building for Mac specifically, the decision to distribute free with no monetization, using Tauri + Rust instead of native Swift (an interesting technical choice they'd have opinions on), and local AI as a product direction.

## Specific work to reference in outreach
Core Intuition's decade-plus of covering the indie Mac scene. Episodes on App Store sustainability and the economics of free apps are directly relevant to Typer's model.
