## Who they are

Gary Rosenzweig is the founder, producer, and host of MacMost - a YouTube channel and website with 476,000 subscribers dedicated exclusively to Mac, iPhone, and iPad tutorials. The channel has been running for well over a decade. Gary is a computer scientist, author of 40+ books on Apple products, and a genuine Mac power user. Content is tutorial-focused: how to get the most from macOS apps and features. Regular cadence (daily or near-daily uploads).

## Why relevant

MacMost is one of the most consistent and trusted Mac tutorial voices on YouTube. Gary's audience is exactly who Typer is built for: everyday Mac users (not developers or enthusiasts) who want to learn how to do more with their machines. A video titled "Run Free AI on Your Mac - No Internet Required" or "How to Use AI on Your Mac Without ChatGPT" would be completely native to his format. He has covered AI tools for Mac before and his audience trusts his recommendations.

## Specific piece to reference

MacMost's format of Mac how-to tutorials - reference his overall channel mission ("how to get the most from your Mac") and pitch Typer as an underexplored Mac feature most of his audience doesn't know exists.
