# Email 1: Intro

Sent: Day 0
Goal: Get a reply. That's it. Not coverage, not a feature. Just a human response.

## Template (v6)

Subject: small thing i made

---

hey [name],

hope you're doing well![optional: one more sentence hook on the SAME line when the hook is real and specific. skip if no honest hook.]

I've been building a small mac only app called Typer. it's a local ai app.

I'm a designer and started messing with local ai at work a while back (wanted something that could process a bunch of customer feedback chats without having to put them in the cloud). it was kind of a fail tbh, but the models kept surprising me, and I ended up thinking local ai should be part of the toolkit for how we use ai.

what blew my mind is that every local ai app out there makes you pick a model family, size, temperature etc.. before you can even chat. it was just screaming at me that there should be an app that works like chat gpt for a common user. one tap to grab the model on first open, no account needed. so that's what I tried with Typer, and it's kind of decent.

anyway, i'm writing cause i'd be really happy if you checked it out, and would love to hear any thoughts on it! I want to continue improving the app :) https://typer.space

Duarte

---

## Copy rules

- Lowercase "ai", "chat gpt", casual throughout. Not "AI" or "ChatGPT".
- No em dashes. No dashes at all. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- No filler words: genuinely, truly, really, honestly, incredibly (except "really happy" in the locked close — that's Duarte's own phrasing).
- The body is LOCKED. Tells the builder story in Duarte's voice.
- The close is LOCKED. Verbatim: "anyway, i'm writing cause i'd be really happy if you checked it out, and would love to hear any thoughts on it! I want to continue improving the app :) https://typer.space"
- Sign "Duarte" (no last name, no title).

## When to add a hook line

Only when it references a SPECIFIC real piece of their work AND there's a genuine connection to Typer. Keep it ONE sentence. Examples that work:
- "saw your Indie App Spotlight on AnywAIr back in December. Typer does sort of the same thing on mac."
- "saw your 'Mac Apps Worth Trying' 2026 post. Typer might fit the next one."

NEVER use "loved your X" or generic praise. Reader can tell instantly.

If you don't have a real piece to reference, DROP the hook entirely. Story-led opens are stronger than fake hooks.

## When Duarte actually knows the person

Replace the hook with a REAL personal memory. Model it on the Dan Shipper opener: "I found Every a few months ago and was super obsessed. When I found the site i basically lost a day cause I just felt I had to read everything." Real sensory detail, not "love your work".

Ask Duarte what he actually remembers before drafting. Don't invent the memory.
