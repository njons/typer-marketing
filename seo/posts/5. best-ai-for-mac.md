There are a lot of AI tools that technically work on a Mac. Most of them are just websites. A few are actual apps. Only one runs entirely on your machine.

---

## What to look for in a Mac AI app

Before getting into specific tools, the things worth caring about:

**Privacy.** Does your data leave your Mac? Most tools send everything to a cloud server. That's fine for casual use, less fine for anything sensitive.

**Limits.** Free tiers on cloud AI tools come with message caps. You'll hit them.

**Offline access.** Can you use it on a plane? In a place with bad wifi? Most tools: no.

**Cost.** Cloud AI subscriptions run $20-$30/month. That adds up.

**Native Mac app vs web app.** A proper Mac app tends to be faster, more reliable, and easier to use than opening a tab.

---

## The main options

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
The most well-known. Has a Mac desktop app now. Requires an account. Free tier has message limits. The best models require a Plus subscription ($20/month). All conversations go to OpenAI's servers.

**Good for:** People already in the OpenAI ecosystem who don't mind the subscription.
**Not good for:** Privacy, offline use, or avoiding monthly costs.

### Claude (Anthropic)
Strong at writing and analysis. Web-based, with a Mac app in beta. Also requires an account. Free tier is limited. Pro plan is $20/month.

**Good for:** Long documents and nuanced writing tasks.
**Not good for:** Offline, privacy, or free use at scale.

### Copilot (Microsoft)
Built into Windows and available on Mac. Free tier is generous. Tied to Microsoft's ecosystem and data practices.

**Good for:** People already using Microsoft 365.
**Not good for:** People who want to keep data off Microsoft's servers.

### Typer
Runs entirely on your Mac. Free, no account, no message limits, works offline. Downloads a capable AI model to your machine - about 500MB. After that, no internet needed.

**Good for:** Privacy, offline use, no-subscription use, people who want AI that just works.
**Not good for:** People who need the absolute latest frontier model for complex reasoning tasks.
