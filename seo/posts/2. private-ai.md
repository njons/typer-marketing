# What Is Private AI - And How to Actually Have It

**Target keywords:** private ai, what is private ai, private ai assistant, private ai chat

---

Every AI tool you've used knows more about you than you probably realise.

ChatGPT stores your conversations. Gemini uses them to improve Google's products. Claude logs your sessions. The trade-off has always been: you get a smart tool, they get your data.

Private AI is the alternative. Here's what it means and how it works.

---

## What is private AI?

Private AI means AI that doesn't send your data anywhere. Your questions, your conversations, your context - none of it leaves your device.

Most AI tools work by sending your input to a company's server, processing it there, and returning a response. That means a third party sees everything you type. Every question. Every document you paste in. Every conversation.

Private AI runs the model locally - on your own computer. There's no server in the middle. Nothing is transmitted. The AI processes your input and responds, all on-device.

---

## Why does it matter?

For most casual questions it probably doesn't. But consider:

- You're working on something confidential and you want help writing it up
- You're asking questions about a health issue before a doctor's appointment
- You're brainstorming something you're not ready to share
- You work with clients whose information you're not supposed to share with third parties

In all of these cases, a cloud AI tool is a liability. A private AI isn't.

There's also the simpler version: some people just don't want a tech company reading their private thoughts. That's a legitimate preference that doesn't require a specific threat model to justify.
